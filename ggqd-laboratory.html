Published AFI2 definition

\[I(x,y)=\frac{\sqrt{x+y}}{2}\left[\frac{\left(\frac yx+\sqrt[3]{x^3+y^3}\right)\sqrt{xe^x+ye^y}}{xy(x+y)^6e^{xy}}\right]^{\frac{1}{x+y}}\]

This follows the algebraically simplified expression in Artificial Fiscal Index: 2-Parameter Deflation. It is not a fitted or modified version of AFI2.

Verification warning. The new quadratic-price baseline is not automatically a monetary price: its units and economic interpretation require further validation. The published AFI2 formula also does not currently reproduce all reported TI-Nspire values. The laboratory calculates the printed expression exactly as transcribed; the discrepancy must be resolved before interpreting results as independent validation of the calculator model.

Model revision

The November 2026 revision replaces the preceding weighted square-root baseline with the product of a coefficient norm and a weighted quadratic price expression. The GG subtraction is unchanged. Earlier comparison outputs retain the original equal-coefficient baseline for reference.

Mathematical scope

The laboratory restricts inputs to n,u≥0 and x,y>0, a conservative positive domain for the original formula. A finite numerical result does not establish that GGQD is an economically valid deflation measure. Currency-unit invariance, an operational definition of universality, and historical out-of-sample testing remain unresolved.
