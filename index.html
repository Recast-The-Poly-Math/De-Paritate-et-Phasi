I

Argumentum

From an elementary identity
to a numerical instrument.

The essay begins with the exact additive form c = a + b. It asks what becomes possible when reconstruction is joined to a carefully defined symmetry: closure can be measured, phase can be tested, and conjecture can be separated from proof.

“The decisive passage is not from number to number, but from an elementary identity to a disciplined instrument of inquiry.”
