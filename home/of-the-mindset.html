Thesis overview

Coordinate Generalization & Prime Number Thesis

Recasting the Functional: A Thesis on Coordinate Generalization and Mathematical Heuristics begins with the generalization of a point. The manuscript calls possible links between coordinate locations virtual space. It treats points in a constructed sequence as actualized when those links are used to build a representation.

The draft develops this idea through a coordinate transformation, a proposed measure of virtual entropy, and an epsilon ratio written as ε = A/P, with A and P named area and parameter. These are concepts within the manuscript’s proposed framework.

A coordinate experiment with primes

The fall 2025 visualization maps the positive integer n to a point in the plane. The logarithm and square root give the horizontal and vertical coordinates different rates of growth.

F(n) = (sin(n) ln(n + 1), cos(n) √n)

Prime labels are computed separately by trial division. Changing the range shows how apparent clusters depend on the coordinates and on how many integers are plotted.

Recast PrimeScope — VisuaLaw

Prime Edge Detector

The original project explored ΔB waveforms, macro density, twin pulses, and an entropy field. The current PrimeScope plots the split of each integer and compares experimental signals with independently verified prime markers.

E(N)
The lower half, ⌊N/2⌋.
B(N)
The upper half, ⌈N/2⌉.
ΔB(N)
The change B(N) − B(N − 1). It equals 1 for odd N and 0 for even N.

For example, 9 and 11 both give ΔB = 1. The verified prime markers distinguish the composite 9 from the prime 11.
